# Organization

## Mission Statement

Our mission is to restore damaged landscapes and empower young people to make a positive impact in the fight against climate change. We work at the intersection of ecology and technology to bridge the gap between older landowners and a new generation eager to protect the planet. This corporation is organized and operated exclusively for charitable purposes within the meaning of Internal Revenue Code section 501(c)(3).

## History

Wildland Ecotech is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit spun off from Wetfish, a for-profit software and hardware development company incorporated in 2017. The conservation work that became Wildland Ecotech began in 2020 when two properties were acquired for restoration: a 45-acre property in Northern Colorado (the Lazy Lambda) and an 18-acre property in Upstate New York (the Fish Pit).

After five years of volunteer-driven restoration work under the Wetfish banner, Wildland Ecotech was formally established as an independent nonprofit to create a distinct brand and organization for the conservation work.

## Financial

### Donation Processing

Stripe is used for payment processing. Three products are configured:

- Wildland Ecotech One-Time Donation
- Wildland Ecotech Monthly Donation
- Wildland Ecotech Annual Donation

A Stripe Payment Link with "Customers choose what to pay" is active for one-time donations. Recurring donation support will be built into the Laravel platform.

### Tax Filing

The organization files annually based on revenue level (990-N, 990-EZ, or 990). The board reviews filing requirements and deadlines at quarterly meetings.
